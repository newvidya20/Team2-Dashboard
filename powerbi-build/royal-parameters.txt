CREDIT DUE DILIGENCE – REVIEW PARAMETERS 
GST, Banking & Financial Analysis Checklist 
1. GST Review Parameters 
• Buyer and supplier list with total sales and purchases. 
• Sales-to-percentage ratio with buyer and supplier. 
• Export details and total export ratio to annual turnover. 
• Filing date along with late fee details. 
• Location-wise, state-wise and revenue breakup. 
• Intra-group transactions. 
• HSN-code-wise product summary. 
2. Banking Review Parameters 
• Internal account transactions. 
• Inflows / outflows. 
• Cheque returns. 
• ECS / NACH returns and penal charges. 
• Number of credit entries. 
• BTO (Banking Turnover). 
• Party-wise inflow / outflow. 
• Loan disbursement and cash deposits. 
• CC utilisation. 
3. Banking & GST Reconciliation (Triangular Report) 
• Party-wise GST vs. Banking reconciliation: Check the amount booked in GST for each party against the corresponding party -
wise credit received in the bank account.
